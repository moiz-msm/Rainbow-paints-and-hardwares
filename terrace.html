What is Terrace & Water Tank Waterproofing?

Terrace waterproofing and water tank waterproofing are essential processes that protect your home from water damage by preventing moisture from entering the building structure. Since terraces and water tanks are continuously exposed to rain, sunlight, temperature changes, and stored water, they require specialised waterproofing systems to ensure long-term durability.

Waterproofing for terrace involves applying protective waterproofing products and coatings over the terrace surface to prevent rainwater from penetrating the concrete. This helps reduce cracks, seepage, and leakage into the rooms below.

Similarly, water tank waterproofing creates a waterproof barrier inside or outside the tank to prevent water leakage, maintain structural strength, and help preserve water quality. Whether it is an overhead or underground water tank, proper waterproofing protects the structure from continuous exposure to water.

Modern waterproofing solutions, such as waterproof membranes, cement-based coatings, and liquid-applied systems, provide reliable protection while extending the lifespan of terraces and tanks. Choosing the right waterproofing products during construction or renovation helps minimise future repairs and protects your home from costly water damage.

 

Why is Terrace & Water Tank Waterproofing Important?

Terraces and water tanks are among the most exposed parts of a building. Heavy rainfall, standing water, sunlight, and regular wear can gradually weaken these structures if they are not properly protected.

Here are some key reasons why roof waterproofing, terrace waterproofing, and tank waterproofing are important:

 

  • Prevents Water Leakage

    • A properly waterproofed terrace prevents rainwater from entering the roof slab and leaking into ceilings, walls, and electrical systems.
     

  • Protects the Building Structure

    • Continuous moisture can weaken concrete and corrode steel reinforcement. Waterproofing helps preserve the structural strength of the building.
     

  • Extends Surface Life

    • Using high-quality waterproofing products protects terrace finishes, concrete surfaces, and water tanks from early deterioration.
     

  • Reduces Maintenance Costs

    • Preventing leaks is more cost-effective than repairing damaged ceilings, peeling paint, cracked plaster, or weakened concrete.
     

  • Maintains Water Quality

    • Proper water tank waterproofing helps prevent contaminants, moisture ingress, and cracks that may affect stored water.
     

  • Improves Weather Resistance

    • A durable waterproof roof coating protects terraces from heavy rainfall, UV exposure, and temperature fluctuations throughout the year.

    Whether for a residential home or commercial property, waterproofing is an investment that helps protect the building and reduces future maintenance expenses.

     

Common Causes of Terrace & Water Tank Leakage

Water leakage usually develops gradually due to ageing materials, poor construction, or lack of maintenance. Identifying the cause early helps prevent more extensive damage.

Some of the most common causes include:

  • Cracks in the terrace slab.

  • Damaged waterproofing layers.

  • Poor drainage leading to water accumulation.

  • Improper slope on the terrace.

  • Expansion joint failure.

  • Low-quality construction materials.

  • Ageing concrete.

  • Plumbing leaks near overhead tanks.

  • Structural movement causing cracks.

  • Poor sealing around pipe openings.

  • Continuous exposure to rain and sunlight.

  • Deterioration of existing waterproof coatings.

Routine inspection and timely repairs can significantly reduce the risk of leakage and improve the performance of waterproofing systems.

 

Signs Your Terrace Needs Waterproofing

Terrace waterproofing problems often become visible long before major structural damage occurs. Recognising these warning signs allows homeowners to take corrective action before repairs become expensive.

 

  • Water Stains and Discoloration

    • Brown stains or dark patches on ceilings beneath the terrace often indicate that water is entering through the roof slab.

    These stains usually become more noticeable during the monsoon and should not be ignored.
     

  • Ponding Water

    • Water that remains on the terrace long after rainfall is known as ponding water. Standing water increases pressure on the waterproofing layer and can eventually cause leakage if left untreated.

    Proper drainage and effective waterproofing for terrace help prevent this issue.
     

  • Cracked or Bubbling Paint

    • Peeling, blistering, or bubbling paint on ceilings or walls below the terrace often indicates hidden moisture caused by roof leakage.

    Repainting alone will not solve the problem unless the waterproofing issue is addressed first.
     

  • Visible Roof Damage

    • Visible cracks, broken concrete, loose tiles, or damaged joints allow rainwater to enter the structure.

    Repairing these defects and applying suitable roof waterproofing products helps prevent further deterioration.
     

  • Interior Mold and Mildew

    • Moisture entering through the terrace can create damp conditions that encourage mould and mildew growth inside the home.

    Besides affecting the appearance of walls and ceilings, mould may also reduce indoor air quality.

    If these warning signs appear, scheduling a professional waterproofing inspection can help identify the source of leakage before extensive repairs become necessary.

     

Signs Your Water Tank Needs Waterproofing

A damaged water tank can result in continuous leakage, water loss, and structural deterioration. Regular inspections help detect problems before they become severe.

 

  • Cracks, Leakage, or Damage

    • Visible cracks on the tank surface or continuous water leakage are clear signs that waterproofing is required.

    Even small cracks can expand over time and weaken the structure.
     

  • Moisture, Dampness, or Mold

    • Persistent moisture around the outside of the tank may indicate hidden leakage.

    If left untreated, this can lead to dampness, mould growth, and damage to nearby walls or ceilings.
     

  • Rust or Corrosion

    • Metal components such as reinforcement bars, pipes, or fittings may begin to rust if water penetrates the concrete.

    Early waterproofing helps minimise corrosion and extends the life of the tank.
     

  • Decreased Water Quality

    • If stored water becomes cloudy, develops an unusual smell, or contains visible particles, damaged waterproofing may be allowing contaminants to enter the tank.

    Maintaining the waterproof lining helps protect water quality.
     

  • Age of the Water Tank

    • Older water tanks naturally become more vulnerable to cracking and leakage due to years of water pressure and weather exposure.

    Periodic inspection and timely waterproofing help extend their service life.
     

  • Ineffective Sealing of Joints and Connection

    • Pipe connections, construction joints, and inlet or outlet openings are common points where leakage begins.

    Proper sealing using suitable waterproofing products helps prevent water from escaping through these vulnerable areas.

Types of Roof Waterproofing

Different roofs require different waterproofing systems based on the building design, weather conditions, and level of exposure to rain and sunlight. Choosing the right roof waterproofing solution helps prevent leaks, protects the concrete, and extends the life of the roof.

Types of Water Tank Waterproofing

Water tanks require waterproofing systems that can withstand constant contact with water while protecting the structure from leakage. The right solution depends on whether the tank is new, old, underground, or overhead.

 

  • Epoxy Coatings

    • Epoxy coatings create a smooth, durable protective layer inside concrete water tanks. They help reduce water absorption and improve the lifespan of the tank.

    These coatings are often used where a clean and easy-to-maintain surface is required.

  • Cementitious Waterproofing

    • SmartCare Damp Proof Ultra

    • SmartCare Cem Boost
       

    • Cementitious waterproofing is one of the most widely used methods for water tank waterproofing. It bonds well with concrete and helps prevent leakage through walls and floors.

    Recommended Asian Paints Products:

  • SmartCare Damp Proof Ultra

  • SmartCare Cem Boost
     

  • Polyurea Coatings

    • Polyurea coatings form a flexible waterproof layer that cures quickly and offers excellent durability. They are suitable for water tanks exposed to demanding conditions and frequent use.
     

  • Bituminous Coatings

    • Bituminous coatings are mainly used on the outer surfaces of underground tanks where protection from groundwater is required.

    These coatings create a moisture-resistant barrier that helps preserve the structure.
     

  • Fibreglass Lining

    • Fibreglass lining provides an additional waterproof layer inside water tanks. It is commonly used when repairing older tanks or improving the durability of existing structures.

     

Step-by-Step Terrace & Water Tank Waterproofing Process

Professional waterproofing follows a planned process to ensure every vulnerable area is properly protected.

 

Step 1: Inspection

The terrace or water tank is carefully inspected to identify cracks, damaged areas, leakage points, and drainage issues.
 

Step 2: Surface Preparation

Dust, dirt, loose concrete, old coatings, and other contaminants are removed to create a clean surface for better product adhesion.
 

Step 3: Crack Repair

Visible cracks and joints are repaired using suitable products such as SmartCare Crack Seal or SmartCare Crack Fill Paste.
 

Step 4: Primer Application

Where required, a primer is applied to improve bonding between the surface and the waterproofing system.
 

Step 5: Waterproofing Application

The selected waterproofing products are applied evenly across the terrace or tank surface. Extra attention is given to corners, joints, drainage outlets, and pipe connections.

Depending on the project, products such as SmartCare Terrace Waterproofing, SmartCare Damp Proof, or SmartCare Hydroloc Xtreme may be used.
 

Step 6: Curing

The waterproofing layer is allowed to dry and cure according to the product instructions to achieve maximum performance.
 

Step 7: Water Testing

For terraces, water is retained on the surface to check for leaks. For tanks, water is filled after curing to confirm the waterproofing system is working effectively.
 

Step 8: Final Inspection

A final quality check ensures complete coverage before the terrace or water tank is put back into regular use.

 

Terrace & Water Tank Waterproofing Cost

The cost of terrace waterproofing and water tank waterproofing depends on the size of the area, the condition of the surface, and the waterproofing products selected.

A terrace with minor cracks may require only surface preparation and waterproof coating, while an older terrace with significant damage may need extensive repairs before waterproofing.

Similarly, the cost of waterproofing a water tank depends on factors such as the size of the tank, existing leakage, repair requirements, and whether the waterproofing is applied internally or externally.

A professional inspection helps determine the most suitable solution and provides an accurate estimate based on the project's requirements.

 

Factors Affecting Terrace & Water Tank Waterproofing Cost

Several factors influence the overall cost of waterproofing:

  • Total area to be waterproofed.

  • Existing cracks and structural damage.

  • Surface preparation requirements.

  • Type of waterproofing products selected.

  • Number of waterproofing coats required.

  • Accessibility of the terrace or tank.

  • Labour and application complexity.

  • Waterproofing method used.

  • Drainage improvements, if required.

  • Warranty provided with the waterproofing system.

Choosing high-quality waterproofing products may involve a higher initial investment but can significantly reduce future repair and maintenance costs.

 

Benefits of Terrace & Water Tank Waterproofing

Proper waterproofing for terrace and water tanks offers long-term protection for your home and helps prevent costly structural repairs.

Some of the key benefits include:

  • Prevents rainwater from entering the building.

  • Reduces roof leakage and ceiling damage.

  • Protects concrete from moisture.

  • Extends the life of terraces and water tanks.

  • Helps maintain clean water storage.

  • Reduces maintenance and repair costs.

  • Protects paint finishes from peeling.

  • Improves resistance to changing weather conditions.

  • Helps prevent cracks caused by continuous moisture.

  • Increases the overall durability and value of the property.

With premium waterproofing products and proper application, you can enjoy reliable protection against water damage throughout the year.

 

Tips to Maintain a Waterproof Terrace & Water Tank

Waterproofing is a long-term investment, but regular maintenance helps keep it effective for years. A few simple checks can prevent small issues from becoming major repairs.

Follow these tips to maintain your terrace waterproofing and water tank waterproofing:

  • Clean the terrace regularly to remove dirt, leaves, and debris that may block water drainage.

  • Keep rainwater outlets and drain pipes free from blockage.

  • Avoid allowing water to collect on the terrace for long periods.

  • Inspect the terrace after heavy rainfall for signs of leakage or cracks.

  • Repair small cracks as soon as they appear to prevent water from entering the concrete.

  • Avoid dragging heavy objects across the terrace, as they may damage the waterproofing layer.

  • Check water tanks regularly for cracks, leakage, or damaged joints.

  • Inspect pipe connections and inlet or outlet points for signs of moisture.

  • Clean water tanks periodically to maintain hygiene and water quality.

  • Schedule a professional inspection every few years to ensure the waterproofing system remains in good condition.

Routine maintenance can significantly extend the life of waterproofing products and reduce the need for major repairs.

 

Common Mistakes to Avoid During Terrace Waterproofing

Even the best waterproofing products may not perform well if they are applied incorrectly. Avoiding these common mistakes helps ensure long-lasting protection.

 

  • Ignoring Small Cracks

    • Hairline cracks may seem harmless, but they often allow water to enter the concrete over time. Repairing cracks before waterproofing improves the overall performance of the system.
     

  • Poor Surface Preparation

    • Applying waterproofing over dusty, wet, or damaged surfaces reduces product adhesion and may lead to early failure.
     

  • Choosing the Wrong Waterproofing Product

    • Different terraces require different waterproofing systems. Selecting products based only on cost instead of the surface condition and weather exposure can reduce durability.
     

  • Skipping Drainage Improvements

    • Even the best waterproofing for roof cannot perform effectively if rainwater remains on the terrace due to poor drainage. Proper slope and clean drainage outlets are equally important.
     

  • Applying Too Few Coats

    • Using fewer coats than recommended can reduce the effectiveness of the waterproofing system and leave areas vulnerable to leakage.
     

  • Not Allowing Proper Curing Time

    • Walking on the waterproofed surface or exposing it to water before it has fully cured can affect the performance of the waterproofing layer.
     

  • Delaying Repairs

    • Ignoring early signs of leakage, peeling paint, or damp ceilings often results in larger and more expensive repairs later.

    Professional application and high-quality waterproofing products help avoid these issues and improve the lifespan of the terrace.

Frequently Asked Questions on Terrace & Water Tank Waterproofing
What are the benefits of terrace & roof waterproofing?

Roof waterproofing and terrace waterproofing help prevent rainwater from entering the building, protect concrete from moisture damage, reduce maintenance costs, improve the lifespan of the roof, and keep interiors free from leaks and damp patches.

 
Why do I need water tank waterproofing for a new home?

Even in a newly constructed home, water tank waterproofing is important. It helps prevent future leakage, protects the tank from continuous water exposure, maintains water quality, and extends the life of the structure.

 
What are the benefits of tank waterproofing for homes/buildings?

Water tank waterproofing offers several advantages, including:

  • Prevents water leakage.

  • Protects the concrete structure.

  • Reduces maintenance costs.

  • Helps maintain cleaner stored water.

  • Increases the lifespan of the water tank.

  • Minimises the chances of cracks developing over time.
How long does roof waterproofing last?

The lifespan of roof waterproofing depends on the waterproofing products used, the quality of application, weather conditions, and regular maintenance. Professionally applied waterproofing systems using premium products can provide reliable protection for many years.

 
Which waterproofing is best for a terrace?

The best waterproofing for terrace depends on the condition of the surface and the level of exposure to rain and sunlight. Liquid waterproofing systems, acrylic coatings, polyurethane coatings, and cementitious waterproofing are commonly used for residential terraces.

 
Which waterproofing is best for a water tank?

For concrete water tanks, cementitious waterproofing and epoxy-based systems are widely used because they bond well with concrete and help prevent leakage. The most suitable solution depends on whether the tank is underground or overhead and its overall condition.

 
How do you waterproof a concrete tank?

Waterproofing a concrete tank generally involves:

  • Cleaning the surface.

  • Repairing cracks and damaged areas.

  • Applying a suitable primer if required.

  • Applying waterproofing products evenly across the tank.

  • Allowing the coating to cure.

  • Filling the tank with water to test for leakage before use.
How do I stop my cemented water tank from leaking?

The first step is to identify the source of the leak. Cracks and damaged joints should be repaired before applying suitable water tank waterproofing products. If the leakage is severe, a professional inspection is recommended to determine the best repair method.

 
How do I avoid leakages from the terrace?

To reduce the risk of terrace leakage:

  • Repair cracks as soon as they appear.

  • Keep drainage outlets clean.

  • Prevent water from collecting on the terrace.

  • Inspect the terrace regularly, especially before the monsoon.

  • Apply quality waterproof roof coating and waterproofing systems when required.

  • Schedule periodic professional inspections.

These simple steps can help maintain effective terrace waterproofing and reduce future repair costs.

 
Is there a difference between underground water tank waterproofing & terrace tank waterproofing?

Yes. Although both aim to prevent leakage, the waterproofing requirements are different because the tanks are exposed to different conditions.

Underground water tanks are surrounded by soil and groundwater, so the waterproofing system must protect the tank from moisture entering from outside as well as prevent water from leaking out.

Terrace (overhead) water tanks are exposed to sunlight, rain, heat, and changing weather conditions. Their waterproofing system should be able to withstand these environmental conditions while preventing water leakage.

A professional inspection helps determine the most suitable water tank waterproofing solution based on the type and condition of the tank.

 
How do you waterproof a water tank?

The waterproofing process usually involves the following steps:

  1. Empty and clean the water tank thoroughly.

  2. Inspect the tank for cracks, damaged joints, and weak areas.

  3. Repair all visible cracks before waterproofing.

  4. Prepare the surface by removing loose particles and dirt.

  5. Apply suitable waterproofing products evenly on the required surfaces.

  6. Allow the waterproofing system to cure completely.

  7. Fill the tank with water and check for any signs of leakage before putting it back into use.

Using high-quality products and following the correct application process helps provide long-lasting protection.

The cost of waterproofing depends on several factors, including:

  • The size of the area.

  • The condition of the terrace or water tank.

  • The extent of crack repairs required.

  • The waterproofing products selected.

  • The application method.

  • Labour and project complexity.

Since every project is different, Asian Paints recommends a professional site inspection to assess the surface and provide a customised quotation based on your specific waterproofing requirements.

 
What is the best waterproofing solution for concrete terraces?

There is no single solution that suits every terrace. The best option depends on the condition of the concrete, the amount of rainfall the terrace receives, and whether it is a new or existing structure.

For most residential concrete terraces, commonly used solutions include:

  • Liquid waterproofing systems.

  • Acrylic waterproof coatings.

  • Polyurethane waterproofing.

  • Cementitious waterproofing.

Asian Paints products such as SmartCare Terrace Waterproofing, SmartCare Damp Proof, and SmartCare Hydroloc Xtreme are designed to provide reliable protection for different terrace waterproofing requirements.

 
Can terrace waterproofing be done without removing existing tiles?

Yes, in some situations. If the tiles are in good condition and the leakage is minor, certain waterproofing systems can be applied over existing tiled surfaces.

However, if the leakage is caused by damaged waterproofing beneath the tiles, loose tiles, or major cracks in the concrete, removing the tiles may be necessary to ensure a long-lasting repair.

A professional inspection can help determine the most suitable solution.

 
What causes terrace seepage during monsoon?

Terrace seepage during the monsoon is usually caused by one or more of the following:

  • Cracked terrace surfaces.

  • Damaged waterproofing layers.

  • Blocked drainage outlets.

  • Poor slope that allows water to collect.

  • Worn-out sealants around joints and pipes.

  • Ageing concrete.

Timely maintenance and proper roof waterproofing help prevent these problems and protect the building during heavy rainfall.

 
How often should terrace waterproofing be redone?

There is no fixed timeline for redoing terrace waterproofing. Its lifespan depends on the quality of the waterproofing products, weather exposure, workmanship, and regular maintenance.

Instead of following a fixed schedule, homeowners should arrange an inspection if they notice:

  • Water leakage after rainfall.

  • Cracks on the terrace.

  • Peeling paint on ceilings below.

  • Damp patches indoors.

  • Water collecting on the terrace for long periods.

Regular inspections help identify issues early and may extend the life of the existing waterproofing system.

 
What is the difference between roof waterproofing and terrace waterproofing?

The terms are often used interchangeably, but there is a slight difference.

Roof waterproofing is a broad term that refers to protecting all types of roofs, including sloped roofs, flat roofs, and terraces.

Terrace waterproofing specifically focuses on flat, accessible roof surfaces that are commonly used for walking, seating, gardening, or utility purposes.

Both solutions are designed to prevent water leakage, but the products and application methods may vary depending on the roof design and level of exposure.

 
Can waterproofing improve the lifespan of a water tank?

Yes. Proper water tank waterproofing helps protect the tank from continuous water exposure, reducing the risk of cracks, leakage, and structural damage.

Some of the long-term benefits include:

  • Better protection against water leakage.

  • Improved durability of concrete.

  • Reduced maintenance and repair costs.

  • Better water storage conditions.

  • Longer service life of the tank.

  • Lower risk of moisture-related damage to nearby structures.

When combined with regular inspections and timely maintenance, quality waterproofing products can help keep your water tank in good condition for many year

Different Types Of Wall Textures for Interiors

When it comes to textured walls there are various types of options available, and here are a few of them that you can explore for your home -

Stone Wall Texture

Stone wall textures mimic the appearance of natural stone, adding a rustic and earthy charm to interiors or exteriors. They can be achieved through techniques like faux painting or using actual stone veneers.

Brick Wall Texture

Brick wall textures offer a timeless and classic look, perfect for adding warmth and character to any space. They can be created with techniques like brick veneer, textured paint, or wallpaper.

Metallic Wall Texture

Metallic wall textures bring a modern and luxurious feel to any room. Using metallic interior texture wall paint or textured interior paint, you can achieve a shimmering effect that catches and reflects light, adding depth and sophistication. This type of internal texture paint is ideal for accent walls or areas where you want to create a focal point.

 

Various Types Of Interior Wall Textures For Different Rooms in Your Home

 

Living Room Wall Texture

For the living room, consider textures like suede finish or metallic textures to add elegance and sophistication, complementing your décor style.

Bedroom Wall Texture

Create a cosy and inviting ambience in the bedroom with textures like soft fabric finishes or subtle patterns, promoting relaxation and comfort.

Bathroom Wall Texture

Opt for moisture-resistant textures like Venetian plaster or textured paint specifically formulated for bathrooms to prevent mold and mildew growth while enhancing aesthetics.

Kitchen Wall Texture

In the kitchen, choose easy-to-clean textures like semi-gloss or eggshell finishes that offer durability and resistance against stains and moisture.

Dining Room Wall Texture

For the dining room, consider using interior texture wall paint to create a visually appealing space that enhances your dining experience. Textured interior paint finishes such as faux finishes, brushed pearl, or stucco can add depth and character to the walls, making the dining area a standout feature in your home.

 

Best Wall Texture Ideas For Your Home Interiors

When considering the best wall texture ideas for your home interiors, textured wall paint emerges as a versatile and impactful choice. With texture paint, you can transform ordinary walls into dynamic focal points, adding depth, character, and visual interest to your living spaces. Here are a few Textured Wall Ideas-

1. Faux Finishes - Utilize techniques like Venetian plaster or suede textures for a luxurious and sophisticated ambience. Mimic the look and feel of natural materials such as stone or leather, adding warmth and elegance to any room.

2. Creative Techniques - Experiment with stencilling or sponge painting to add personalized patterns or designs to your walls. Unleash your creativity and incorporate artistic elements for a unique and eye-catching effect.

3. Highlight Architectural Features - Use textured paint strategically to accentuate architectural elements like archways, alcoves, or columns. Create focal points by adding texture to specific areas of your home, enhancing visual interest and depth.

4. Accent Walls - Designate one wall as a focal point and apply textured paint to create a dramatic statement. Choose contrasting colours or bold patterns to make the accent wall stand out and add personality to the space.

5. Play with Color and Finish - Experiment with different colours and finishes to enhance the visual impact of textured wall paint. Opt for subtle neutrals for a calming atmosphere or bold hues for a vibrant and energetic look.

6. Texture on Ceilings - Extend texture paint to the ceiling to add dimension and visual interest to the entire room. Consider techniques like stippling or popcorn texture for a unique overhead design.

7. Blend with Existing Decor - Coordinate the texture paint with your existing decor and furnishings to create a cohesive look. Ensure that the chosen texture complements the overall style and theme of your home interiors.

8. DIY Projects - Explore DIY texture painting projects for a cost-effective and personalized approach to home decoration. Get creative with tools and techniques such as ragging, combing, or sponging to achieve custom textures.

 

FAQS
What are the most popular wall texture techniques?

One of the most popular textures for walls is the "orange peel" texture. This textured wall paint is characterized by a subtle dimpling effect on the surface, resembling the texture of an orange peel, hence its name. It provides a smooth yet visually interesting finish that helps to conceal minor imperfections in the wall surface. Orange peel texture is versatile and can be applied to both interior and exterior walls, making it a popular choice for many homeowners and decorators.
How to decide which wall texture paint works best for your walls?

Consider factors such as the room's purpose, desired aesthetic, existing décor, and maintenance requirements when choosing wall textured wall paint.
How is wall texture paint different from ordinary paint?

Textured wall paint contains additives like sand or silica to create texture, offering a tactile surface unlike ordinary paint, which provides a smooth finish.
Which colours work well with textured paints?

Choose colours that complement your overall décor scheme and evoke the desired mood or atmosphere in the space, ensuring harmony and visual appeal.

 
Is textured paint a good idea for my home?

Textured wall paint is an excellent idea for adding visual interest, hiding imperfections, and enhancing durability in high-traffic areas, making it a popular choice for homeowners seeking versatility and style.
What is the cost of applying textured paint?

The cost of applying textured interior paint depends on various factors, including the type of texture wall paint, surface preparation, and labour costs. You can use our paint budget calculator to get an accurate estimate based on your specific needs.
Can textured paint be applied over existing paint?

Yes, texture wall paint can be applied over existing paint. Ensure the surface is clean, dry, and free of debris. Priming may be necessary for optimal adhesion and a smooth finish with indoor textured paint.
How long does textured paint last?

Indoor textured paint typically lasts 5-10 years, depending on factors like surface preparation, application quality, and environmental conditions. Regular maintenance can extend its lifespan.
How do I maintain and clean textured walls?

To maintain and clean walls with internal texture paint, use a soft brush or vacuum to remove dust. For stains, a mild detergent solution and gentle scrubbing with a sponge can help keep the texture of wall paint looking fresh.
